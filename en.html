Privacy Policy — Basira | Digital Balance

Last Updated: May 17, 2026

Introduction

We at Basira | Digital Balance ("we", "the App", "Basira") respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains clearly and transparently what data the App accesses, how we use it, and who sees it.

Core principle: Basira is a fully offline-first application — all your data stays on your device and is not sent to any external servers, except in the very limited cases described below.

Target audience: This app is designed for a mixed audience of all ages, age 6 and up, including students, teenagers, and adults. The App is safe for use by children — it collects no personal data, shows no ads, and contains no external links to unsafe content.

1. Data the App Accesses

1.1 Notification Content

  • What is accessed: Notification titles, content, and the sender app name.
  • Why: To classify notifications (important / routine / defer) and apply smart filtering based on your mode (Focus, Sleep, etc.).
  • How it is used: Processing happens locally on your device only via NotificationListenerService. We do not send any notification content to the cloud or any server.
  • Storage: The log of blocked notifications is stored locally in an encrypted Room database on your device, and is automatically deleted after 30 days.

1.2 Screen Content via Accessibility Service

  • What is accessed: Screen names and UI element identifiers of other apps (e.g. TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) when they are opened — solely to detect short-video pages (Shorts/Reels).
  • Why: To prevent opening addictive content pages and display the Intervention Screen.
  • What we do NOT access or read:
  • We do not read private chats or messages.
  • We do not read passwords or login data.
  • We do not record anything the user types.
  • Storage: No screen content is stored. Processing is immediate and temporary, in memory only.

1.3 Contacts

  • What is accessed: The contacts list — names and phone numbers only.
  • Why: To let you define a "Family Allowlist" whose notifications are always allowed.
  • How it is used: Only the contacts you manually select are stored locally. We do not upload your contacts to any server.

1.4 Location

  • What is accessed: Approximate (fine when needed) coordinates and your city.
  • Why: To compute prayer times accurately, fully locally (using a local calculation algorithm, e.g. Umm al-Qura).
  • How it is used: The location is used a single time to look up your city and compute prayer times. Only the city name is stored locally (not the coordinates). We do not send your location to any server.

1.5 Usage Stats

  • What is accessed: Time spent in different apps (open and use durations).
  • Why: To display usage reports and enforce time limits you yourself set.
  • How it is used: All processing is local and is not shared with any third party.

1.6 List of Installed Apps

  • What is accessed: The names of apps installed on your device.
  • Why: To display them in the "App Management" list so you can choose which ones to filter notifications for or limit usage of.
  • How it is used: The list is displayed locally only and is not sent anywhere.

2. What We Do Not Do

  • ❌ We do not collect your personal data (name, email, phone, etc.) — the App requires no login.
  • ❌ We do not track you through any external analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • ❌ We show no ads and share no data with ad networks.
  • ❌ We do not sell your data to any third party, neither for money nor for free.
  • ❌ We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • ❌ We do not read your chat content on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, or anywhere else.

3. Where Is Your Data Stored?

All your data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Room database (protected by Android's sandbox): for notification logs, session settings, Quran progress, etc.
  • DataStore: for settings and preferences.
  • Android cloud backup: applied according to defined rules (see data_extraction_rules.xml) — basic settings are backed up but sensitive notification logs are not.

5. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share your data with any third party. The App does not contact any external server by default.

Exceptions: - Google Play Services: Used only to obtain approximate location efficiently (if you grant permission). This service is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. - Android Backup: Android may store some app data in your Google account if you have backups enabled in your system settings. This is outside our control and is governed by Google's policies.

6. Data Security

  • All local data is protected by standard Android security mechanisms (Sandbox).
  • No external API endpoint receives anything.
  • We recommend enabling screen lock (PIN/biometric) on your device to protect your data.

7. Your Rights

As a Basira user, you have the right to: - Delete your data: Uninstalling the App immediately removes all your local data. - Revoke permissions: You can revoke any permission at any time from system settings. - Inquire: For any question or request, contact us (see section 10).

8. Children's Safety

Basira is safe for use by children (ages 6 and up) as part of its mixed-audience reach, in line with the following standards:

8.1 We collect no personal data from children (or any user)

  • No account, no login, no name, no email, no phone number.
  • We do not use the Advertising ID or any other persistent identifier.
  • No data is sent to any external server.

8.2 No ads, no tracking

  • The App contains absolutely no advertising.
  • We have no relationship with any ad network or third-party marketing service.
  • We do not use external analytics or crash-reporting tools.

8.3 Safe content

  • The App contains no external links to unsafe content.
  • All in-app content (dhikr, Qur'anic verses, wisdom quotes, alerts) is in Arabic/English and is modest and suitable for all ages.
  • There is no inter-user messaging and no user-generated content from other users.

8.4 Compliance with children's privacy laws

We comply with: - COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) — United States, for users under 13. - GDPR-K (Article 8 of GDPR) — European Union, for users under 16. - Google Play Families Policy — Google's requirements for apps directed at children or mixed audiences.

11.4 Position summary

Because Basira collects no personal identifiers, uses no external servers, and transfers no data across borders, most obligations of these laws (cross-border transfer, access requests, deletion-on-request, etc.) simply do not apply to us. Nevertheless, we maintain full transparency through this policy and give you complete control to revoke any permission or uninstall the app at any time.

One-line summary: Basira runs entirely locally on your device and sends none of your data anywhere.
